## [Build tools, to build more](https://www.bensbites.com/p/build-tools-to-build-more)
*🍔 Ben's Bites | 2026-06-04*

Hey folks,

I'm making progress on my agents manual! I think I finally figured out how I want the thing to look and feel. 

I've built and rebuilt this damn thing so many times in this process, which is actually part of the process. I am a lazy workaholic (h/t Rick Rubin) - I have to spend time in the work, even if it feels like it's not going anywhere, until 'suddenly' things click. 

Whilst in the process, you find yourself wanting tools to exist to make things easier for yourself--that's a huge part of why learning agents and how to steer them is so good. 

You can build tools to enable you to build things. 

I spun up this tool before bed last night where I can comment/delete on copy whilst I'm building, which I copy as one big block as agent feedback.

  * **[Codex has two new additions: Plugins and Sites](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-every-role-tool-workflow/)**. Plugins are pre-built collections of skills, connectors to relevant apps (like Figma for designers) and instructions tuned for specific roles like data analysis and product design. [Sites](https://x.com/_simonsmith/status/2062168863400235023) lets users create a shareable website/app with a database, file storage, env vars, access controls, and more. Initially only available to business and enterprise users.

  * A bunch of new open models released recently -

    * **[Gemma 4 12B](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemma-4-12b/)** \- Multimodal (i.e. accepts images and audio as input) and performs nearly as well as the two-month-old 26B variant.

    * **[Ideogram 4.0](https://ideogram.ai/blog/ideogram-4.0/)** \- 9.3B model for image generation. Trained on JSON prompts for control over the layout, colours and text for each element on the image. Also check [Reve 2.0](https://blog.reve.com/posts/the-layout-bet/) for the focus on layout of elements in an image (but it's closed-source).

    * **[Miso One](https://x.com/AodenTeoMT/status/2062204362102100295)** \- 8B text-to-speech model claiming expressive speech with 110ms latency.

    * Also, just like Cursor's Composer, more companies are trying out fine-tuning big open-weights models for their domain-specific work. Latest entry -> **[Harvey](https://x.com/harvey/status/2062218656420167785)** got a Kimi 2.6 agent to beat Opus 4.7 on its legal benchmark at ~11x lower cost.

  * **[Microsoft Scout](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/02/introducing-microsoft-scout-your-always-on-personal-agent/)** is an always-on Microsoft 365 agent built on OpenClaw (reminder: openclaw is open-source).
