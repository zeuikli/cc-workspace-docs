## 全文

Wrote up my initial impressions of Claude Fable 5 - it has a big model smell: slow, expensive and capable of crunching through pretty much everything I threw at it https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/9/claude-fable-5/

TL;DR from my testing:
- First-token latency ~7-8s vs Opus's 4-5s (that blank pause feels long)
- Once it starts: dense, not slow
- Handled my worst context-length tests without degradation
- Correctly identified edge cases in 3 separate debugging sessions that Opus missed
- The "big model smell" is real: it over-explains, it double-checks, it caveats

Worth it? For anything I'd spend more than 30 mins debugging manually: yes, absolutely.
