## Path One: The Distribution Play

**The lowest barrier path that most people underestimate.**

### The concept

Pick 1-2 AI skills/tools and master them.

Think: Claude, OpenClaw, AI front-end development, agentic workflows, prompt engineering.

- For beginners: broad skills like Claude and prompt engineering.
- For advanced/technical: agentic workflow design, AI systems, etc.

Pick a trending AI skill/tool and go DEEP - learn it better than 99.99% of people. Then document the journey publicly.

Share your findings consistently on distribution platforms like X, YouTube, or wherever your audience already is.

The content does not need to be perfect, but it does need to be **specific, honest, and useful to someone one step behind you.**

### How to monetise

Once you have built genuine expertise and a small but engaged audience:

1. **Paid Community or Membership** - private group sharing workflows, systems, templates
2. **Course or Cohort** - package everything into a structured learning experience
3. **Sponsors** - companies pay for your reach and marketing campaigns

### Content advice (from someone who built a $20M+ agency)

- Pick a skill/tool with longevity - avoid tools that become obsolete (many n8n experts learned this the hard way)
- **Topic → Hook**: most important thing is TOPIC. Focus on viral topics, then optimise the hook
- Consistency is king
- Optimise per platform: YouTube = authority/evergreen; X = personality + real-time news
- Be genuine - don't lie, share real experiences
- Learn storytelling and engaging copy
- Network: reach out to creators, get collabs, reply to big accounts

## Path Two: Consulting - The SMBs Play

**The fastest path to real income for most people.**

Demand is enormous; supply of people who can actually deliver is tiny.

### The concept

You become the AI person for businesses that cannot afford to hire one full-time.

1. **Diagnose** where AI can create the most leverage in their specific operation
2. **Design** the solution
3. **Charge** for the implementation

Target: local law firms, marketing agencies, real estate offices, accountancy practices, e-commerce businesses, professional services - companies relying on outdated manual processes.

### Business model

1. **Audit fee** - initial fee to diagnose their problems
2. **Solution fee** - for building and deploying the automation/agent/AI solution
3. **Maintenance/Retainer** - fee to maintain and update the solution

### Advantages

- Low competition - very few small businesses have been pitched AI
- Outsource - if not technical, hire others to build automations; focus on sales and scaling
- Research tools like Manus can scan for local clients: "I live in [x], find customers in my area that fit [xyz criteria]..."

## Path Three: The Product Play

**The highest-ceiling path. The hardest. The one that requires most before giving anything back.**

### The concept

Build something people pay for: AI-native tool, automated service, productized workflow, or agency built on AI infrastructure.

**Most important principle: PMF (product-market fit) comes FIRST - always.**

You should only pursue this if you know exactly what you're building, who it's for, and ideally already have distribution and a network to back it.

### Three focus areas

1. **Vertical AI tools** - software for specific industry problems that couldn't exist two years ago. Examples: AI-powered trading journals, automated compliance monitoring, AI content pipelines for media companies.
2. **AI-powered agencies** - high-ticket service businesses built on AI infrastructure
3. **Productized services** - package a service deliverable via AI at a fixed price. Sell the output, not your time.

AI has compressed time and capital required to test products: for free, in a day, you can code a prototype, launch a website, and run a marketing campaign.

### Advice for launching successful products

- **Distribution is king** - don't build for 3-6 months then realize you have no distribution
- **Marketing → Build** - attack marketing and PMF first, THEN build
- **Creator Economy** - outsource distribution by hiring creator/influencer network

## 研究摘要

**TL;DR**：AI 致富三條路線（Distribution→Consulting→Product），作者以 $20M+ 實績背書，重點是「先搶分發，再建產品」，PMF 優先於 building。

### 核心論點

- 三架構師框架：Career / Income / Venture Architect（Income + Venture = 本文焦點）
- Path 1 Distribution：先掌握 1-2 個 AI 工具，公開記錄學習過程，再貨幣化（社群/課程/贊助）
- Path 2 Consulting：瞄準無法承擔全職 AI 員工的 SMB，收審計費 + 方案費 + 月費

- 核心原則：Distribution 是護城河，先獲得分發能力再建產品；n8n 等工具過時風險是真實警告
