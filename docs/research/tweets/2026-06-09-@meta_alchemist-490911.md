## 全文

A tip for Claude users who are excited about Fable 5:

Claude Fable 5 is SOTA across the board, with a large margin, and I love that

BUT from the 23rd, it will only be available through credits, instead of being able to use the model fully with a plan.

Use these 14 days on your best projects

Giving it primarily audit and architecture-related work, so that you'd have great documentation on how to improve everything you've been building, instead of trying to put it towards tasks that will eat your limits.

As long as Fable focuses on knowing what to improve, other models can help you do the rest.

## 研究摘要

**TL;DR**：Fable 5 在 6/23 轉為 credit-only 前有 14 天窗口——應優先用於審計與架構（高智力密度/低 token 量），讓較便宜模型執行實作。

### 核心論點

1. **時間窗口策略**：6/9–6/23 共 14 天 Fable 5 含在 plan 內，之後需額外付費
2. **Audit > Build**：Fable 的最高 ROI 用途是審計與架構文件化，而非 token 密集的 build 任務
3. **模型分工**：「Fable knows what to improve, other models do the rest」= Think/Review (Fable) + Build (cheaper) 分工

### 關鍵數據 / 工具

- 14 天免費窗口（6/9–6/23）
- 策略：audit + architecture documentation → 用較便宜模型執行改善

### 批判性觀察

1. **與 JJEnglert Think→Build→Review 完全吻合**：本推文更簡潔地表達了相同策略——frontier 模型做判斷/審計，cheaper 模型做執行
2. **直接對應 workspace 實踐**：`/fable-pilot`（重構/優化/稽核）+ `/sonnet-pilot`（品質實作）正是此策略的實體化；core.md「Effort 先於 model」也呼應了精打細算的精神
3. **隱含 Anthropic 定價策略資訊**：Fable 5 從 plan-included → credit-only 的轉換時程（14 天後）是重要商業情報
