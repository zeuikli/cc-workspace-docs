## 全文

Graph engineering vs Loop engineering:

Graph engineering is deciding where the work is allowed to go. You lay out the steps, the rules, who has to approve what, and the safety checks. It keeps things controlled and predictable.

Loop engineering is making the work get better each time it runs. The agent does the task, checks its own result, and tries again a little sharper. It keeps things improving.

Graph is the rails. Loop is the motor.

Rails keep you from crashing. The motor is what actually moves you forward.

Build the loop first when you want a task to keep improving on its own.

Add the graph once the work has handoffs, approvals, or steps you can't afford to get wrong.

Strong agents need both.

## 研究摘要

**TL;DR**：用「軌道 vs 引擎」的比喻把 Graph Engineering（控制工作能去哪、誰要核可、安全檢查）與 Loop Engineering（讓同一任務一輪比一輪更好）分開定位，主張兩者互補而非互斥，先建 loop 求進步、有 handoff/核可/高風險步驟時再加 graph 求可控。
